What services does BAWARY provide?

BAWARY provides senior pharmaceutical Quality Assurance support including batch record review, batch-release documentation support, deviation investigations, root cause analysis, CAPA management, GMP audits, inspection readiness, QMS remediation, supplier/CDMO auditing, quality risk management and interim QA support.

Can BAWARY support a short-term QA backlog?

Yes. Short-term project support can be used for defined backlogs such as batch documentation review, deviations, CAPAs, audit preparation or other operational QA tasks.

Can BAWARY work remotely?

Yes. Remote support is available globally where the nature of the assignment permits remote execution. Germany, the Netherlands and Belgium are key service regions for on-site and hybrid support.

Does BAWARY work with CDMOs and suppliers?

Yes. Services include supplier and vendor auditing, CDMO/CMO auditing, vendor qualification, CAPA follow-up and external quality oversight.

Who can contact BAWARY?

Pharmaceutical and biotechnology companies, CDMOs, CMOs, QA leaders, QPs, manufacturing organizations and consulting or recruitment firms can contact BAWARY regarding suitable QA assignments.
